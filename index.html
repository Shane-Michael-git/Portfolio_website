Hi, I'm Shane Michael!

Welcome to my about me page. Here you'll learn a little bit about me and why I want to work towards being an engineer. I was a personal trainer for years and also spent some time working on computer programming as a hobby.



I'm 28 years old and currently employed full time as a shift supervisor at Starbucks in Paoli, Pa. I'm currently working on my BSE in Electrical Engineering. I hope to one day use my education in engineering to work with robotic prosthetics. I was able to teach myself most of the poular coding languages as well as learning about biomechanics for personal training, I think these skills combined with a better knowledge of electrical systems will give me a leg up in my desired field. I play guitar and have 9 cats and a new Samoyed puppy.



I am joining this course to gain a better understanding of the engineering process to give myself a solid foundation for future learning. With engineering I love the allure of fixing problems, in particular I'm drawn to robotic prosthetics as they solve the problem of losing limbs, once they are sufficiently advanced that is. As a musician I couldn't imagine living without the dexterity required to perform my instrument. Beyond my desire to improve robotic prosthetics, particularly hand dexterity, I'd love to improve the robotics field as a whole. Furthermore I'd enjoy developing more advanced techniques to create computer chips or even helping design more robust power grid systems. Everyone in the world has a problem they need solved, engineers solve those problems. Engineering betters the world and each individual's life.



I am taking pathway to admission classes so hope to pass with all A's and be formally accepted into ASU, if not fully into my desired major path of Electrical Engineering. To consider myself an engineer, I will need to have the prerequisite knowledge to solve real world problems, in some ways that means I already am an engineer, not a formal paid one, but I engineer solutions on a daily basis. I hope to be getting my master with an accelerated master program through ASU in 5 years. My philosophy is journey before destination. We all die one day, what matters is the steps you take on the path. the first step to the masters program is actually being accepted though. To me success is passing college at the moment.
